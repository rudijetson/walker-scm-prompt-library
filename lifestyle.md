# Lifestyle / Personal Prompts

Simple, low-stakes prompts to get comfortable with AI on your own life before you bring it to work. No Walker or client data needed — use any approved tool. Pick one and try it on something real this week.

## Weeknight Meal Planner

**Use when:** You want to stop the daily "what's for dinner" scramble.

**Data & tool rule:** Any approved AI tool — personal use. No Walker or client data needed.

```text
You're a practical home cook helping me plan dinners.

Plan [5] weeknight dinners for [2 adults and 2 kids]. Each should take under [30] minutes and use simple grocery-store ingredients. I want them [budget-friendly and kid-friendly], and I need to avoid [list any allergies or foods you dislike].

Give me:
1. The 5-dinner menu
2. One combined grocery list, organized by store section
3. Any prep I can knock out ahead on Sunday
```

## Trip Planner

**Use when:** You're planning a getaway and don't want to spend hours researching.

**Data & tool rule:** Any approved AI tool — personal use. No Walker or client data needed.

```text
Help me plan a [long weekend] trip to [destination] for [who's going].

Budget level: [modest / mid-range / treat-ourselves]
We're into: [food, easy hikes, history, just relaxing — pick yours]

Give me a day-by-day outline with a couple of options each day, rough costs, one or two local spots most tourists miss, and a short list of what to book in advance versus what to decide once we're there.
```

## Say-It-Better Personal Message

**Use when:** You're staring at a blank screen on a message that's hard to get right.

**Data & tool rule:** Any approved AI tool — personal use. Leave out anything truly private.

```text
Help me write a [thank-you note / apology / kind way to decline / check-in with a friend going through something hard].

The situation: [describe it in a sentence or two].

Make it warm and genuinely like me — not stiff or formal. Keep it [short]. Give me two versions with slightly different tones so I can pick the one that fits.
```

## Explain It Simply

**Use when:** You're curious about something but every explanation online assumes you already get it.

**Data & tool rule:** Any approved AI tool — personal use. Great for public topics.

```text
Explain [topic you're curious about] to me like I'm smart but brand new to it.

Use plain language and a real-world analogy, skip the jargon, and keep it to a few short paragraphs. At the end, give me 3 things I could look into next if I want to go deeper.
```

## Easy Routine I'll Actually Keep

**Use when:** You want to start a small healthy habit without overhauling your life.

**Data & tool rule:** Any approved AI tool — personal use. (Not medical advice — check with a doctor before starting something new.)

```text
Help me build a simple [walking / stretching / home workout] routine I can actually stick to.

I've got about [20] minutes, [3] days a week, [at home], and I'm a [beginner]. My goal is [more energy / less back stiffness / general fitness].

Give me a specific Week 1 plan — what to do each day — and how to make it a little harder once it starts feeling easy. Keep it doable; I'd rather start small and not quit.
```

## Simple Monthly Budget Helper

**Use when:** You want a clear picture of where your money goes, minus the lecture.

**Data & tool rule:** Any approved AI tool — personal use. These are your personal finances, not Walker's.

```text
Be a friendly, non-judgmental budgeting helper.

My monthly take-home pay is [$____]. My regular monthly expenses are roughly: [rent/mortgage $__, car $__, groceries $__, utilities $__, subscriptions $__, other $__].

Help me build a simple monthly budget: show me roughly where my money is going by category, point out one or two places I could adjust if I wanted to save more, and suggest a realistic amount to set aside each month. Keep it encouraging.
```
