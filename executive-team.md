# Weekly Ops Performance Narrative

**Use when:** Turning a raw internal KPI summary into a Monday executive briefing narrative for leadership review

**Data & tool rule:** Any approved AI tool. Keep inputs to Walker-internal info — no client names, manifests, or account data.

**⚡ Easy version — just type and go:**

> `Write a short, plain-English summary of this week's operations numbers for leadership: [paste the numbers]`

**Detailed version — when you want the full structured output:**

```text
ROLE:
Walker SCM Operations Manager drafting the weekly executive briefing — no client names, EINs, or account-specific detail in scope

CONTEXT:
You are preparing the weekly operations performance narrative for Walker SCM's executive team. The summary below contains internal KPIs only — entry volumes, CBP exam rates, clearance cycle times, warehouse throughput, on-time delivery %, open exception counts, and headcount notes. No client names or account-specific data.

[PASTE WEEKLY KPI SUMMARY HERE]

TASK:
Write a 250–350 word executive briefing narrative with four labeled sections (see Output Format). Lead with the overall health vs. prior week, call out two or three notable performance movements with a plain-language cause, flag any risk or bottleneck requiring a decision, and name one recommended focus for the coming week. Use direct C-level language; flag data gaps explicitly rather than papering over them.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Four labeled prose sections — no bullet-point walls:
1. Headline — one sentence on overall operational health vs. prior week
2. Notable Movements — two or three performance shifts with likely cause
3. Risks & Decisions Needed — any bottleneck or issue requiring executive action
4. Recommended Focus — one priority for the coming week

Suitable for copy-paste into a Monday morning leadership email or Teams message.
```
