# Draft Prospect Outreach Email

**Use when:** Drafting a cold or warm outreach email to a new import/export prospect before sending

**Data & tool rule:** Microsoft Copilot ONLY. Client/importer data must stay inside Walker M365 — never paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, NotebookLM, or Perplexity. Output is client-facing — a Licensed Customs Broker must review it before it is sent.

```text
ROLE:
Business development writer for Walker SCM — a licensed U.S. customs brokerage, freight forwarder, and 3PL — skilled at translating operational capabilities into concise, peer-level prospect communications

CONTEXT:
Walker SCM is a licensed U.S. customs brokerage, freight forwarder, and 3PL headquartered in the United States. Services include ISF filing, customs entry preparation, HTS classification consulting, ocean/air freight forwarding, and 3PL warehousing with ground transport. Differentiators: licensed broker staff on every account, proactive compliance support, and a single point-of-contact model.

Prospect details (company name, industry, known import/export activity, qualifying notes):
[PASTE PROSPECT DETAILS HERE]

Walker service angle or pain point to lead with (optional):
[PASTE SERVICE ANGLE OR PAIN POINT HERE, OR LEAVE BLANK]

TASK:
Write a short outreach email (under 150 words) to the prospect above. Open with a specific, relevant observation about their business or industry — not a generic compliment. Connect it to one concrete Walker SCM capability that addresses a likely pain point for that type of importer or shipper. Close with a low-friction call to action (a 15-minute call or a single reply question). Do not mention pricing, fees, or specific client names. Bracket any assumption made about the prospect so the sender can verify before sending.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
One ready-to-send email with subject line. Plain prose — no bullet lists inside the email body. After the email, one line listing any bracketed assumptions for sender review.
```
