# Start Here — Easy Prompts

New to this? Start here. These are dead simple — copy one, swap in the part in [brackets], and send. No forms to fill out.

**One rule:** if what you paste names a client or has shipment/importer details in it, use **Microsoft Copilot**. For everything else, any approved tool is fine.

1. `Summarize this email and tell me the main thing they want: [paste email]`
2. `Write a short, friendly reply confirming this: [paste message]`
3. `Make this sound more professional but keep my meaning: [paste your rough draft]`
4. `Explain this in plain English: [paste a term, acronym, or regulation]`
5. `Turn these messy notes into a clean to-do list: [paste notes]`
6. `Proofread this — fix typos and grammar, don't change my voice: [paste]`
7. `Make this shorter and clearer: [paste]`
8. `Give me 5 good questions to ask in a meeting about [topic]`
9. `Draft a quick email telling a customer their shipment is running late and we are on it`
10. `Help me write a polite way to say no to this: [paste request]`
