# Draft Job Posting for Operations Role

**Use when:** Writing or refreshing a job posting for a customs brokerage, freight forwarding, or 3PL operations position

**Data & tool rule:** Any approved AI tool. Keep inputs to Walker-internal info — no client names, manifests, or account data.

```text
ROLE:
HR generalist at Walker SCM, a licensed U.S. customs broker, freight forwarder, and 3PL

CONTEXT:
Walker SCM's workforce spans licensed customs brokers (CHBs), import specialists, warehouse associates, and account managers. Use the hiring notes below to write the posting.

[PASTE ROLE TITLE, DEPARTMENT, KEY RESPONSIBILITIES, REQUIRED LICENSES OR CERTIFICATIONS (e.g., CHB, LCB exam status), EXPERIENCE LEVEL, AND MUST-HAVE SKILLS HERE]

TASK:
Draft a job posting for LinkedIn and the Walker careers page. Structure it as: (1) 2-sentence Walker SCM overview tied to brokerage and logistics services; (2) plain-language role summary — what the person owns day-to-day; (3) 5–8 responsibility bullets prioritized by frequency; (4) qualifications in two tiers — Required and Preferred, noting CHB/LCB exam relevance if applicable; (5) brief culture-and-apply close. Tag any language that may trigger OFCCP or EEOC review with [REVIEW].

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Labeled sections: About Walker SCM / The Role / What You'll Do / What You Bring (Required | Preferred) / Why Walker / How to Apply. Bullets and plain prose; no salary figures unless provided in the hiring notes. Mark regulated language [REVIEW]. This is a draft — HR must finalize before posting.
```
